# Findings from drafting the ADRs, 2026-11-04

## Outcome

On 2026-11-04 the owner, Mikael Axelsson, accepted [ADR-0001](0001-record-architecture-decisions.md), [ADR-0008](0008-simulation-fidelity.md), [ADR-0019](0019-test-framework.md) and [ADR-0020](0020-fixed-step-clock-and-numerics.md). In the same change, C1 to C5, T1, T4, T5, T6 and G1 to G5 were corrected in the GDD and `CLAUDE.md`, and T3 was corrected in part, so the statement below that no document has been changed, and the proposed corrections in the tables, describe the state when this register was drafted. D4 and D13 are settled by ADR-0020 and [ADR-0005](0005-three-layer-split.md): ADR-0020 measures delay in integer ticks and gives `sim/` its own generator, and ADR-0005 sets the line for RefCounted. T2, D1 to D3 and D5 to D12 remain the owner's open choices. T3's question of how a changed source is detected also remains open, and the GDD now says so. T7, T8 and T9 concern `.claude/` files, lie outside the GDD correction and remain open. The original items and their evidence follow unchanged as the record of what was found.

## Findings

Writing the ADRs meant reading the GDD, `CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/` against each other and against official documentation, and that turned up places where they disagree. None of these documents has been changed, because each correction is either the owner's call or changes what the game teaches. Under [ADR-0001](0001-record-architecture-decisions.md) each item stays a defect until the change that resolves it corrects every document involved, and this register then records the outcome.

A second agent re-checked most of these items against the cited lines and the official documentation. It refuted none and narrowed some, and the wording below reflects the narrower version. Items G4, T7 and D10 to D13 did not go through that re-check and rest on the drafting agents' reports, although an independent reviewer repeated the piped test behind T7. Line numbers refer to the files as committed in `6055b25`. External documentation was read by agents on 2026-11-04 and has not been entered in `content/facts.json`, which does not exist yet, so these sources still need a fact-sheet entry when the correction is made.

## Wrong or imprecise claims about Claude

These matter most, because a wrong rule teaches a wrong exam answer. The GDD's wording would be built into the simulation and the tooltips.
