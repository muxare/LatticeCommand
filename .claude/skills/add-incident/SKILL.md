---
name: add-incident
description: Scaffolds a game incident from a scenario and answer key. Use when adding a new incident to content/.
---

Interview the user before writing anything. Ask for the scenario and colony type, the domain and task statement, the correct fix and the tempting wrong fixes with their Concord doctrines.

Then delegate the draft to the content-writer subagent with those answers as a structured brief, and run the fact-check skill on the result. Report the incident as awaiting human approval, because AI drafts and humans sign off.
